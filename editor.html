Tools
Enter switch
Blocks
Left click: left button block, right click: right button block.
Shadows
Click to choose the shadow for the left button. The right button removes shadows. Shadows 1–3 are the ones used by the auto shadows.
Selected object
Nothing selected. Click a light, steam or crate with its tool.
Level settings
seconds, 0 = none
Random crates
These crates are put at random places when the level starts. Crates placed on the map with the crate tools come in addition.
